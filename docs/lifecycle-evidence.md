The PR #39 record also describes a later read-only real-Clerk identity/history check, with its retained older backend and source scope explicit. Neither observation is silently promoted to a fresh current-provider or production pass.

The service-backed browser checks zero cumulative microphone requests across its fresh documents, no unexpected external product/provider traffic, allowed diagnostics, native-origin ownership and bounded cleanup. It asserts exact provider context and persisted identities rather than accepting any nonempty answer. These facts support reconciling the standalone deterministic text lifecycle clause without asserting that the fixture tests external Clerk login screens.
